THE PROBLEM // WHY RGB-D BROKE DOWN

The Specular Trap on Industrial Metal Pallets

In autonomous warehouse logistics, heavy-payload Automated Guided Forklifts (AGFs) must dock their forks into tight pallet pockets with millimeter tolerance. Active RGB-D cameras failed on specular steel pallets due to infrared scattering, saturation, and catastrophic depth dropouts.

THE BREAKTHROUGH // MONOCULAR HCCEPose

Dense Gray-Code Maps & 4-Way PnP Consensus

Adapted HCCEPose to monocular RGB input with dense per-pixel Gray-code coordinate bit-planes, rejecting glare-corrupted pixels by consensus and resolving 180° axial pallet symmetry through quadrant voting.
