THE PROBLEM // DECOUPLED CONTROL DRIFT

Why Traditional Mobile Manipulation Fails

Decoupled systems command the arm via an IK planner while a separate controller maneuvers the base. In high-speed dynamic tasks, this creates extreme tracking lag, base-arm oscillations, and workspace singularities.

THE BREAKTHROUGH // UNIFIED 18-DOF POLICY

End-to-End Coordinated Whole-Body Policy

A single neural policy receives full-body proprioception and directly outputs 18 joint position offsets at 50 Hz. The quadruped base bends, crouches, and steps automatically to expand the arm's reach.
