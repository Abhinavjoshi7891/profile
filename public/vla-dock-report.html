Closed-Loop Diagnoses That Unlocked 94% Reliability

DIAGNOSIS 01 · 41% → 66%

Hiding Velocity Eliminates Copycat Causal Confusion

Analysing failure videos revealed the policy stalled whenever it stopped: observing near-zero traction wheel velocity led it to predict continued zero velocity ("stationary, therefore stay stationary"). Completely withholding velocity proprioception forced the model to infer motion intention from sequential visual observations and steering angle, surging success by +25 percentage points.

DIAGNOSIS 02 · 66% → 94%

Privileged Expert Redesign for Decisive Recoveries

The original expert triggered subtle, ambiguous micro-corrections whenever lateral error drifted by 2 cm. Because the monocular camera could not resolve such subtle offsets, the cloned policy became hesitant. Broadening alignment bands to pocket tolerance (4 cm / 3°) and instituting decisive, high-yaw-rate pivot maneuvers boosted closed-loop performance to 94% (120 / 128 starts) with zero jams.

MODEL ARCHITECTURE

SmolVLA + 98M Flow-Matching Action Expert

Operates at 20 Hz from 256×144 scaled RGB images. Truncated SmolVLM2 (16 layers, 350M parameters) processes image tokens and the frozen instruction prompt "dock into the pallet". A dedicated 98M parameter action expert generates continuous 2-DoF control commands [steering angle target δ, wheel speed u] via conditional flow matching.
